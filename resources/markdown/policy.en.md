# **Privacy Policy**
## **Data Collection:**
- **Personal Information:** Names, email addresses.
- **Payment Data:** Credit or debit card information.
- **Browsing Data:** IP address, browsing history, cookies.
## **Purpose of Processing:**
- **Bookings and Trip Management:** Processing reservations, issuing tickets, managing changes and cancellations.
- **Communication:** Sending confirmations, updates, personalized offers.
- **Marketing:** Conducting marketing and advertising campaigns.
## **Legal Basis for Processing:**
- **Consent:** Obtaining explicit consent from the user for the processing of their data.
**Contract Performance:** Processing data to fulfill the booking made.
- **Legitimate Interest:** Using data to improve services and personalize the user experience.
## **Data Recipients:**
- **Service Providers:** Taxis, hotels, etc.
- **Data Processors:** Companies that provide payment, marketing, etc. services.
- **International Transfers:**
Informing users if data will be transferred to countries outside the EU and the security measures implemented.

## **Data Subject Rights:**
- **Access:** The right to know what personal data is held.
- **Rectification:** The right to request the correction of inaccurate data.
- **Erasure:** The right to request the deletion of data.
- **Objection:** The right to object to the processing of data.
- **Restriction:** The right to request the restriction of processing.
- **Data Portability:** The right to receive personal data in a structured format.
- **Data Security:**
Technical and organizational measures to ensure data security and prevent unauthorized access.
- **Data Retention:**
Data retention periods.
- **Children:**
Indicating whether data is collected from children and how parental consent is obtained.

## **Cookies:**
Explaining what cookies are and how they are used.
- **Changes to the Privacy Policy:**
Informing the user of any changes to the policy.

- **Contact:**
Providing contact information for the user to exercise their rights.
